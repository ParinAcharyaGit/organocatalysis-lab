2021 Nobel Prize in Chemistry

Why does this research matter?

No metals needed

Catalysts are composed from carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, sulphur and phosphorus, addressing metal catalysts' sensitivity and environmental impact.

Naturally occuring

Proline is an amino acid, available in both mirror-image forms.

Steps that chain together

Cascade reactions skip purification between steps, as in the vitamin E synthesis, producing less chemical waste.
